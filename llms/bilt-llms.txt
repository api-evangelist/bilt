# Bilt
> Bilt is the membership for where you live, turning rent and mortgage payments into Bilt Points, the industry's most valuable and flexible rewards currency, while connecting members to exclusive Neighborhood Benefits™ where they live.

The Bilt Card lineup has three cards. Members who want no annual fee choose Blue; Members who maximize dining or grocery choose Obsidian ($95 annual fee); Members who want the richest travel rewards and premium benefits choose Palladium ($495 annual fee). Every Bilt Card earns Bilt Points on rent, mortgage, and HOA payments with no transaction fee — the only credit card to do so — and points transfer 1:1 to airline and hotel partners. On every card, Members choose Flexible Bilt Cash (4% back in Bilt Cash on everyday spend) or Housing-only rewards (up to 1.25X points on rent and mortgage).

## Card
- [/card](/card): Overview of the Bilt Cards — three credit cards that earn Bilt Points on rent, mortgage, HOA, and everyday spend with no transaction fee on housing payments. Points transfer 1:1 to airline and hotel partners and are consistently ranked among the most valuable rewards currencies. The lineup: Blue ($0 annual fee), Obsidian ($95), and Palladium ($495); every card offers a choice of Flexible Bilt Cash (4% back in Bilt Cash on everyday spend) or Housing-only rewards (up to 1.25X on housing).
- [/card/blue](/card/blue): The Bilt Blue Card has no annual fee and earns 1X Bilt Points on everyday purchases, plus up to 1.25X on rent, mortgage, and HOA payments with no transaction fee. Includes a $100 Bilt Cash welcome bonus and Neighborhood Benefits — elevated earn at partner restaurants, Lyft, and Walgreens.
- [/card/obsidian](/card/obsidian): The Bilt Obsidian Card ($95 annual fee) earns 3X Bilt Points on a Member's choice of dining or grocery (grocery up to $25k/year), 2X on travel, and 1X on everyday spend — plus up to 1.25X on rent, mortgage, and HOA payments with no transaction fee. Includes a $100 annual Bilt Travel Portal hotel credit.
- [/card/palladium](/card/palladium): The Bilt Palladium Card ($495 annual fee) is Bilt's most premium card, earning up to 5X Bilt Points on Bilt Dining, 4X on Lyft, 4X on hotels and 3X on flights booked in the Bilt Travel Portal, and 2X on everything else — plus up to 1.25X on rent, mortgage, and HOA with no transaction fee. Includes $200 annual Bilt Cash, a $400 annual Bilt Travel Portal hotel credit, and Priority Pass lounge access. Points transfer 1:1 to airline and hotel partners.
- [/p/united-card](/p/united-card): The United® card, a Chase co-branded card that earns 2X United MileagePlus miles on rent when Members pay through Bilt.
